### Q1: "How does your AI detect anomalies without falsely accusing genuine projects?"
**Winning Answer:**

Instead of a black-box binary 'fraud' label, our engine computes a 5-factor additive risk score (0–100) decomposed into budget variance, milestone delay, payment velocity, spatial density, and missing evidence. Every score provides full provenance back to source e-SAKSHI records, and the 'What-If' simulator allows officers to see how submitting missing geo-tagged photos resolves the risk score instantly."*

### Q2: "How do you detect Project Splitting?"
**Winning Answer:**  
> *"Under Section 4.5 of the MoSPI 2023 Guidelines, splitting a single composite asset into micro-works under ₹50 Lakh to avoid higher technical sanctions is prohibited. We run a spatial DBSCAN clustering algorithm combined with temporal co-occurrence: if multiple road or building recommendations occur within a 500-meter radius and 14-day window awarded to related entities, the system flags the cluster for mandatory unified DPR scrutiny."*
